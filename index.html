About

Calm pages, clear ideas.

I began in magazine design and stayed for the discipline it teaches: hierarchy, rhythm and respect for the reader. Today I help studios and founders turn dense material into pieces people finish.

My process is simple. Read everything, sketch loosely, set type early, then edit until only the essential remains.

  • Print trained, digital fluent
  • Writer first, decorator never
  • Accessible type as standard
